The Method

The Process
in Four Steps

The same framework runs through every lesson, livestream and trade review.

01

Environment First

In or out, then long or short. If the market is going to chop you up, being bullish or bearish doesn't matter.

02

Story → Chart → Catalyst

Find strong stocks in leading themes with a clear catalyst ahead. A chart won't get off the couch without something to look forward to.

03

Aggressive Defense

Know your invalidation before you buy. Size so one bad trade can't hurt you. Your stop is just the entry fee.

04

Lazy Offense

Once it's green, let it work. Trail your stop and let the market decide when a winner is done. Green can always get greener.

Magnitude of correctness > frequency of correctness.— Luc
